\chapter{Research Objectives}
\label{chap:objectives}

The overall aim of the proposed research is to investigate whether
LLM-generated continuous optimization heuristics can operate effectively
when fitness evaluations are stochastic. Three research questions will
structure the investigation.

\subsection*{Research Question 1}

\begin{quote}
\textit{How can continuous optimization benchmarks be systematically
extended with controlled stochastic evaluation to study optimization
under fitness noise?}
\end{quote}

An evaluation setting will be established to study the effects of
fitness noise in a controlled manner.
A wrapper layer will be developed over functions from the noiseless
BBOB suite. Established benchmark definitions and literature on
optimization under uncertainty will guide the introduction of
controlled statistical noise
\cite{Hansen2009Noiseless,Hansen2009Noisy,Jin2005}.
The environment will provide a repeatable setting for evaluating
generated heuristics and established optimization methods using noisy
fitness observations, while retaining access to the underlying clean
objective for assessment. Applying noise to the same underlying BBOB
functions, rather than using the separate noisy-BBOB suite, will allow
a direct comparison of AAD performance under noiseless and noisy
evaluations.

\subsection*{Research Question 2}

\begin{quote}
\textit{Can Large Language Models autonomously synthesize continuous
optimization heuristics that are resilient to noise?}
\end{quote}

The experiments will investigate whether LLM-based algorithm synthesis
can produce heuristics that perform effectively when fitness evaluations
are noisy. Existing work has demonstrated that LLaMEA can
generate competitive optimization algorithms for noiseless benchmark
functions \cite{LLaMEA2024}. The framework will be applied to a
representative set of continuous optimization landscapes using
lightweight and cost-effective language models. Rather than manually
introducing noise-specific mechanisms into an optimizer, the experiments
will examine whether such mechanisms emerge through evolutionary
code generation and feedback. Changes in the generated algorithms
over successive generations will be analyzed alongside their performance
to assess whether these strategies are effective in the tested conditions.

\subsection*{Research Question 3}

\begin{quote}
\textit{How robust are LLM-generated heuristics across different noise
conditions compared with established optimization methods?}
\end{quote}

The generated heuristics will be compared with established continuous
optimization methods to assess their performance, robustness, and
generalization. Algorithms generated
under noisy and noiseless conditions will be evaluated across different
noise settings, including noiseless evaluations. This comparison will
examine whether exposure to noise during algorithm evolution leads
to differences in performance and whether the generated heuristics
remain effective when evaluation conditions change.
