%==============================================================================
\chapter{Returned-Solution Success Rates by Configuration}
\label{app:benchmark_success_rates}
%==============================================================================

This appendix reports all 1,920 native champion conditions from
Chapter~\ref{chap:experiments}. Each cell corresponds to one selected champion
for a fixed model, synthesis mode, function, dimension, prompt strategy, and
noise level. Its value is the percentage of 20 terminal trials whose returned
solution has clean optimality gap $\Delta f\leq10^{-8}$.

All values are percentages in increments of five: 100 denotes 20 successful
trials out of 20, 95 denotes 19, and 0 denotes none. Confirmed execution failures
remain in the denominator as unsuccessful trials. A zero rate at this strict
threshold does not imply an absence of search progress.

Each table fixes the model, mode, and dimension, and separates the four noise
levels into distinct blocks. Synthesis and evaluation noise are matched in
these native conditions; the champion may differ between noise levels.
No cell pools trials across functions, dimensions, or noise levels.
These are descriptive point estimates. Per-condition Wilson intervals are
calculated in the reliability diagnostics but are omitted here for readability;
these tables do not report aggregate bootstrap intervals.

The complete grid contains three models, two modes, four dimensions, four
noise levels, five functions, and four strategies. It covers 38,400 terminal
outcomes. The independent evaluation allowance is $10{,}000D$, with instances
1--20 and the trial settings described in Section~\ref{sec:independent_benchmark}.
